Kopilotti Sales for partners

Bring AI-assisted price negotiation to your automotive platform

Kopilotti Sales lets AI handle the customer conversation while deterministic rules keep every price decision under dealer control. It is designed to extend automotive marketplaces, dealer platforms and digital retail systems without replacing their core workflow.

Who it's for

Built to extend the platforms around the dealer

  • Automotive marketplaces

    A potential partnership path is to place a controlled negotiation step alongside vehicle listings while the wider transaction remains in the marketplace or dealer systems.

  • Dealer platforms, DMS and CRM providers

    Extend inventory, deal and customer-relationship tooling with a bounded, server-side price-negotiation step.

  • Automotive digital retail platforms

    Add a negotiation step to an existing online buying journey, ahead of checkout.

  • Valuation and trade-in providers

    A dealer's trade-in and valuation step sits alongside the same price-negotiation decision — a natural, later integration point as a pilot deepens.

  • Finance, leasing and checkout platforms

    Once a price is agreed, the customer moves into the dealer's own checkout and financing process — a natural point for a deeper integration as a partnership deepens.

Proven today

What the current solution demonstrably does

  • AI — a language model — holds a bounded conversation with the customer.
  • The backend makes a deterministic accept, counter-offer, reject or escalate decision.
  • The dealer controls the negotiation rules and boundaries.
  • Exceptional cases can be escalated to a person.
  • Kopilotti does not receive, hold or transfer the customer's funds.

Current onboarding model

How a partnership starts today

  1. A vehicle-specific Magic Link: one opaque link per vehicle carries the negotiation.
  2. Managed in Kopilotti Admin: the dealer already defines the price negotiation rules and how each negotiation progresses.
  3. A bounded pilot first: deeper integration is scoped only once the model has shown its value.

Integration path

The path ahead, honestly

Programmatic integrations are planned and scoped with each partner. Connecting Magic Link creation directly to a marketplace, DMS or CRM platform is on the roadmap, not available today. Kopilotti Sales does not currently offer a public, self-serve API; onboarding runs through Kopilotti Admin, and a deeper technical integration is a deliberate later step, agreed under a partner agreement.
