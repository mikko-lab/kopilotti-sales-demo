Privacy notice

This page covers the Kopilotti Sales presentation and demonstration site (app.kopilotti.online).

What data is collected

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to track visitor numbers and page views. The service does not use cookies and does not build a persistent profile linked to a specific individual. Vercel Web Analytics may process, among other things, the page URL, the referring site, filtered URL query parameters, a coarse geographic location (for example, country), and browser, operating-system and device information. A visitor identifier is computed per request and is not retained for more than 24 hours.

Purpose of use

Data is used solely to understand the site's functioning and popularity. Data is processed by Vercel Inc. as the site's analytics provider; it is not sold and is not used for marketing.

Demo and personal data

This site's price-negotiation demo (vehicle.html) is an illustrative example, and its interactive interface is presented in Finnish. It does not process real personal data, real payment transactions, and does not lead to an actual transaction. Data entered in the demo (for example, a price offer) is not stored permanently and is not linked to the user's identity.
