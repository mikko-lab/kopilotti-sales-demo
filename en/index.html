Kopilotti Sales

Controlled digital car sales, beyond the lead.

Kopilotti Sales helps dealers and automotive platforms progress customers from interest and negotiation toward a verified transaction, while commercial decisions remain under dealer control.

Challenge

Interest peaks when no one is there to answer it

  • Buyers browse cars in the evenings, at night and on weekends — outside dealer opening hours.
  • Today's digital car buying journey often stalls at a lead form, a manual price negotiation, or a handoff to separate financing and contract systems.
  • Interest is highest at the exact moment a salesperson or trade-in manager is unreachable.
  • That delay can break the buying decision entirely.

Kopilotti Sales is built to shorten the time between a customer's offer and the dealer's controlled response.

Solution

One connected path from interest to a verified transaction

Kopilotti Sales flow

  1. Interest
  2. Controlled negotiation
  3. Agreed transaction
  4. Trade-in and financing handoff
  5. Contract and payment progression
  6. Verified delivery readiness

The conversational interface interacts with the customer. Dealer-defined rules and verified external states control the transaction.

Built for

A transaction layer for the platforms and groups around the dealer

  • For marketplaces

    Keep more of the transaction within the marketplace ecosystem instead of handing the highest-value customer activity entirely to dealer-owned channels.

  • For dealer platforms

    Extend inventory, CRM and dealer-management systems with a controlled customer-facing transaction flow.

  • For dealer groups

    Let customers progress outside opening hours while the dealer retains control of pricing, stock, financing relationships and vehicle delivery.

Process

How it works

  1. 1

    Customer selects a vehicle

    Kopilotti Sales opens per vehicle, from a listing page or the dealer's existing sales flow.

  2. 2

    Customer starts the price conversation

    The digital salesperson holds a natural conversation with the customer and captures an offer.

  3. 3

    The system checks dealer-defined boundaries

    The commercially critical decision is made server-side. Price boundaries are never stored in the browser or left for a language model to decide.

  4. 4

    Customer receives a response

    The outcome can be an accepted offer, a controlled counter-offer, ending the negotiation, or escalating the case to the dealer.

Transaction layer

Price decisions belong in the digital journey, before checkout

E-commerce already handles vehicle discovery, identification, contracting and payment. Kopilotti Sales adds the missing step: the customer's offer, a bounded negotiation, and a deterministic price decision.

Current digital path

  1. Inventory
  2. Condition report
  3. Fixed online price
  4. Identification
  5. Contract
  6. Payment

Kopilotti Sales

  1. Inventory
  2. Condition report
  3. Offer + negotiation
  4. Deterministic price decision
  5. Checkout
  6. Contract
  7. Payment

Kopilotti Sales forms the agreed price and hands the customer to the dealer's own checkout, contract and payment process. Funds move directly from the customer to the dealer. Kopilotti does not receive, hold or transfer funds. The dealer confirms payment.

Trade-in and settlement

Trade-in is part of the same deterministic transaction decision

Trade-in V1 handles vehicle identification, an external valuation estimate, an offer, and the settlement amount as separate, traceable steps.

Vehicle valuation provider

Designed to support provider integration, not yet connected in production

The trade-in flow is designed for a provider-hosted vehicle valuation integration; the production connection is subject to a partner agreement and credentials.

  • A provider-independent domain boundary and adapter structure are in place.
  • A missing or failed valuation is routed to manual review — the system never guesses a value.
  • Going live with a specific provider requires a licensed agreement, documentation, credentials and contract testing.

Production boundary: the trade-in and deal-summary structures are implemented and tested, but the trade-in path is not yet part of this public demonstrator's production traffic.

Distinction

What sets Kopilotti Sales apart from a chatbot

Typical chatbot

  • Answers general questions
  • Routes to customer service
  • Retrieves information from a knowledge base
  • Does not participate in price decision logic

Kopilotti Sales

  • Operates inside a specific vehicle's purchase context
  • Runs a bounded price negotiation
  • Uses commercial boundaries the dealer has defined
  • Can continue the conversation outside sales-staff hours
  • Escalates exceptions to a person

Kopilotti Sales does not need to replace the dealer's existing chat solution — it handles one bounded, commercially critical moment.

Lightweight onboarding

A pilot starts with a single link

Kopilotti Sales can go live without a multi-week integration project. The dealer adds one Negotiate the price link to a vehicle page, and deeper integrations are built only once the model has proven its value.

  1. 1

    Vehicle-specific magic link

    The link carries only a random, opaque token. Example address: sales.kopilotti.online/n/8F3KD91X. Vehicle data and negotiation rules are fetched from the server — a floor or target price is never added to the URL.

  2. 2

    Kopilotti Admin

    The dealer sets the list price, the lowest acceptable price, counter-offer steps, campaigns and a validity window. Admin generates the link and a ready-to-use HTML snippet for the vehicle page.

  3. 3

    API and integrations

    Once the model is validated, link creation can be automated through DMS, e-commerce and CRM integrations, and through marketplaces' own APIs and partnership terms.

Control

The dealer sets the negotiation boundaries. Kopilotti Sales operates only inside them.

A dealer can define negotiation terms, for example:

  • Per vehicle
  • Per vehicle group
  • Per price band
  • Based on a campaign
  • Based on stock position
  • As requiring manual approval

The model used is agreed during the pilot, matched to the dealer's own pricing process.

Safety and responsibility boundaries

AI can support the conversation. Verified rules and provider states control the transaction.

  • The dealer controls the commercial rules — price boundaries and counter-offer logic are configured by the dealer, not inferred by the language model.
  • The system does not independently grant credit or make financing decisions.
  • Financing applications are intended to be handled by the financing provider, in the provider's own environment.
  • Financing approval is not the same as contract completion or delivery authorization.
  • The service does not receive, hold or transfer customer funds.
  • Regulated steps — identity verification, credit assessment, payment execution — are handed to the responsible external provider.
  • Unresolved or exceptional cases are stopped or escalated to a person rather than resolved by guesswork.
  • Customer-facing AI output cannot override verified transaction states.
  • Commercial decisions and their basis are recorded traceably.
