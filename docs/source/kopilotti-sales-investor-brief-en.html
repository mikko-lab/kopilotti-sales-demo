Kopilotti Sales

Turn price negotiation into a digital sales channel.

Kopilotti Sales extends the used-car buying journey into the moment a buyer is ready to negotiate. The LLM handles language. A deterministic server-side policy engine makes the commercial decision.

01

PROBLEM

Negotiation is still a manual gap

Search, finance and checkout can be digital, but a serious buyer often still waits for a salesperson or trade-in manager before the price can move.

02

PRODUCT

A bounded negotiation path

Vehicle-specific entry, controlled offer rounds and deterministic outcomes. Cases outside the automated boundary go to a person instead of a guessed answer.

03

WHY IT MATTERS

AI without pricing authority

Natural-language UX can move fast without delegating margin decisions to a model. The dealer defines the rules; the software applies them consistently.

EVIDENCE, NOT CLAIMS

What the repositories support today

PUBLIC DEMO

Negotiation core is live as a demonstrator

Magic Link and dealer-defined deterministic policy are public. Persistent PostgreSQL negotiation sessions, tenant-scoped access, and schema/readiness gates are implemented and tested. No broad production-ready claim.

DEALER TOOLING

Admin path is more than a mockup

Dealer Admin has a validated generic CSV / DMS-export import path with dry-run preview, human approval and atomic apply. No live vendor-specific DMS connection is claimed.

BLACK-BOX EVAL

Independent behavior checks exist

A separate HTTP/API harness runs live scenarios against pinned local Sales and disposable PostgreSQL. Targeting fails closed to non-production and evidence is redacted. Validation, not certification.

BUILT / TESTED

Expansion work exists outside public traffic

Trade-in orchestration, DealSnapshot and provider-independent valuation/history boundaries have implementation evidence. No active VIS / Autovista or carVertical production connection or partnership is claimed.

WHY THIS CAN COMPOUND

The advantage is operating discipline, not LLM novelty.

Deterministic policy, tenant boundaries and isolated evaluation form a reusable transaction layer. Pilot first; integrate deeper after value is proven.

FOUNDER EDGE20+ years in used-car commerce; about 15,000 vehicles bought/sold online.

NON-NEGOTIABLE BOUNDARY

Kopilotti does not handle customer funds or make financing decisions.

Payment, financing and handover stay with the dealer and its finance partner. Live DDN verification and live VIS/history-provider connections remain future integration work.
